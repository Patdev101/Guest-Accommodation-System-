1Overview

A web system that runs guest check-in, reservations, check-out, billing and room status for company accommodation.

Role What they do
Guest Creates an account, books advance reservations online, views reservations, bills and reminders.
Reception Checks guests in and out, books for walk-ins, verifies guests, holds IDs, calls guests, records payments, updates room status.
Admin Sets up locations, rooms, inclusions and rates; manages settings, users and reports.

Stack: Laravel (PHP) + Inertia.js + React + Tailwind CSS, database Microsoft SQL Server.

Project folder: C:\projects\shogun\GuestAccomodation  ·  Database: guest_accommodation

2Room and location setup

Everything about rooms is entered manually by the Admin. Nothing is hard-coded.

  • Location: Villa, Barracks, or any new location the Admin adds.
  • Room (under a location):
    • Name or number
    • Pax capacity: maximum persons in one booking
    • Inclusions: e.g. 2 double decks, fridge, chairs and table
    • Description
    • Current status (the physical state of the room; see section 7)
  • Rates (several per room): a name, a price and a unit. Units are Per hour, Overnight, Day tour, or a custom one the Admin adds. Prices are entered manually by the Admin; the system does not calculate them.
  • Extension rates: the price charged for extending a stay is also entered manually by the Admin, per room.
  • Maintenance records: date, issue, action taken, done by. A room can be set to Under maintenance at any time an issue is reported, not only after a check-out, and the Admin can set an idle room to Out of service.
One booking per room. A room is never shared between separate guests. A group (for example a family of 4) may book a room with pax of 4 or more.

3Check-in process

A guest reaches a room only after passing verification, being assigned a room that is Available, and surrendering a valid ID.

Figure 1Check-in process
  1. Visitor, guest or contractor arrives at the clubhouse.
  2. Reception attends to them.
  3. The guest fills out the check-in form, including a contact number (used for the 1-hour call) and the number of persons (pax).
  4. Verification (internal company process). The system records only the result and who verified.
    • Failed: the guest cannot proceed. The attempt is logged as a verification attempt, even though no stay is created.
  5. Reception checks for a room (Guest Villa, Barracks or another location) with enough pax capacity.
    • No room available now: no ID is taken. The system shows the earliest available slot and a calendar. Continue under Reservations.
  6. Only once a room is confirmed, the guest surrenders a valid ID. Reception records the ID type and number.
  7. Reception assigns the room and hands over the key.
  8. The guest proceeds to the room. The room becomes Occupied.
An expected check-out date and time is required at check-in. The availability estimate and the 1-hour reminder depend on it.

4Reservations

A reservation holds a room for one guest at a chosen date and time. A room can be Occupied now and still be reserved for a later time.

Figure 2Reservation

Who books

  • Advance reservation: the guest creates an account and books online.
  • Walk-in: reception books on the guest's behalf.

Choosing the slot

  1. The system shows the earliest available slot and an availability calendar.
  2. The guest either reserves the earliest slot, or picks their own date and time from the open slots.
  3. Every reservation records the number of persons (pax). It cannot exceed the room's pax capacity.
  4. After an occupied room's current stay, the room is not bookable until its guest confirms they are not extending (or checks out). Earlier reservations stay valid. The next guest enters only after inspection and cleaning.

Cancellation

Guests can cancel their own bookings, and reception any booking, at any time. Payments go through the refund process (section 6).

Payment when reserving (optional)

Option Result
Full payment Paid
Downpayment, any amount Partially paid balance shown at check-in and check-out
No payment Unpaid

No-show

  • The room is held for 1 hour after the reserved time.
  • After 1 hour, reception may mark it No-show, which frees the room. A late guest gets it if still free.
  • Payments are refunded: Full by default, an Admin setting until the final policy is decided.

5Check-out reminder and extension

One hour before the expected check-out, the guest is notified and called. Their answer immediately updates availability for everyone else.

Figure 3Check-out reminder and extension
  1. 1 hour before check-out: the system sends a notification to the guest's account and alerts reception.
  2. Reception calls the guest on the contact number from the check-in form and asks whether they will extend.
  3. Not extending: the room opens for reservation from the expected check-out time. The guest proceeds to check-out.
  4. Extending: reception enters the new check-out time, and the system checks the room's next reservation.
    • No reservation after this stay: extension allowed; availability recalculated.
    • Room reserved for Guest B: another room must be able to take Guest B at the same reserved time (same or larger pax). Guest B is notified and asked for consent to move. Reception can also record Guest B's answer from a phone call.
      • Guest B agrees: Guest B is moved and the extension is allowed.
      • Guest B declines: the extension is denied.
    • No other room for Guest B: extension denied.
Every extension is logged: old time, new time, price (from the Admin's extension rate), who approved, when, and Guest B's consent if one was asked.

6Check-out, ID custody, billing and refunds

Check-out is recorded first and the guest may leave; the room is inspected afterwards. The surrendered ID is returned only when every charge is settled, whether billed to the company or the guest.

Figure 4Check-out process
  1. The guest or contractor reports to reception.
  2. Check-out verification: match the guest to the active stay.
  3. Record the check-out in the guest log. The guest may leave. The room moves to Inspection.
  4. Room inspection: check for damages and unpaid charges.
  5. Bill charges, if any.
    • Billed to the company by default, or to the guest if reception switches it.
    • Fully paid: go to step 6.
    • Not yet paid: the ID stays at reception as Held – pending payment until the company or the guest settles, including damages.
  6. Return the surrendered ID.
  7. The room moves to Cleaning, or to Under maintenance if repairs are needed.

Final bill = room charges + extensions + damages and extras − payments already made (downpayments included).

Refunds (for example, a cancelled reservation with a downpayment) go through a refund process: Requested → Processing → Refunded, with who handled each step and when.

7Room status flow

The room status is the physical state of the room. Reservations are tracked separately, so an Occupied room can still be reserved for a later time. A room returns to Available only after reception marks cleaning or maintenance as done.

Figure 5Room status flow
Status Meaning Moves to Trigger
Available Ready for a guest Occupied, Under maintenance or Out of service Guest checks in (walk-in or reservation), an issue is reported, or the Admin takes it out of service
Occupied Guest is in the room Check-out Guest reports to reception
Check-out Check-out in progress Inspection Check-out recorded
Inspection Staff inspecting the room Cleaning or Under maintenance Inspection done
Cleaning Being cleaned Available Reception marks it done
Under maintenance Being repaired Available Reception marks the repair done
Out of service Not in use for a longer period Available Admin returns it to service

Reserved is not a room status; the room board shows it from reservations (e.g. "Occupied · reserved from 14:00"). Cleaning buffer: Admin setting for estimating when a room will be ready, default 0 minutes.

8Notifications

The guest gets in-app notifications, and reception gets alerts for every upcoming room event.

Event Guest Reception
Reservation confirmed, changed or cancelled In-app notification Listed on the dashboard
Request to move to another room (another guest wants to extend) In-app notification asking Guest B to agree or decline Alert; reception may record the answer from a phone call
1 hour before check-out In-app notification Alert: "Call Room X", plus a phone call to the guest
Upcoming check-in (reservation arriving) In-app notification Alert for the upcoming room
Reservation 1 hour past its time (no-show) — Alert: may release the room
Room cleaning done / room ready In-app notification if they are waiting for it Room board updates
Extension approved or denied In-app notification Logged

Every reminder and call is logged: type, time sent or called, result (extend or check out).

10Open items and build phases

Two business rules still have placeholder defaults. Everything else is ready to build.

Open items

  • Cleaning/inspection buffer time: Admin setting, defaults to 0 minutes until the real time is known.
  • No-show refund: full or partial is undecided; Admin setting, defaults to Full refund.
  • Production server: development uses the local SQL Server Express; the live server is decided later.

Build phases

  1. Foundation: project setup, MSSQL connection, login, the three roles.
  2. Admin setup: locations, rooms, inclusions, rates, maintenance, settings.
  3. Reservations: availability calendar, earliest-slot finder, online and walk-in booking, downpayments.
  4. Check-in and check-out: verification, ID custody, inspection, damages, billing, refunds.
  5. Room board and notifications: live room status, 1-hour reminders and calls, extension rules.
  6. Reports: guest log, held IDs, unpaid company bills, occupancy.
