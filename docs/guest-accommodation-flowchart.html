Guest AccommodationCheck-in & Check-out Process Flow

How guests, reception and the system work together, from arrival or online booking through extension, check-out and room turnover.

Who acts: Guest Reception System (automatic) Shapes: Decision Allowed On hold Stopped / denied Continues in another flow
1

Check-in process

Arrival to room

Required at check-in: contact number, pax and expected check-out time. The ID is taken only once a room is confirmed. Failed verifications are logged.

2

Reservation

Online or walk-in booking

Payment is optional, with no minimum downpayment. Guests and reception can cancel anytime; payments on a cancellation or no-show are refunded (full by default) through Requested → Processing → Refunded.

3

Check-out reminder & extension

1 hour before check-out

Guest B must agree to move before the extension is allowed. Every reminder, call and extension is logged: old time, new time, price, who approved it, and when.

4

Check-out process

Billing & ID custody

Charges are billed to the company by default (or to the guest if reception switches it). The surrendered ID is returned only when the company or the guest has settled everything, including damages.

5

Room status flow

Lifecycle of every room

Reservations are tracked separately from room status, so an occupied room can still be reserved for later. After the current stay, a room opens for booking only once its guest confirms they are not extending.

Key rules

One booking per room

Rooms are never shared between separate guests. Pax is the maximum number of people in that one booking.

ID stays until paid

The guest may leave with charges unpaid, but reception keeps the ID until the company or the guest settles the bill.

1-hour reminder

The guest gets an in-app notice and a call from reception. Their answer updates availability for everyone.

1-hour no-show

A reserved room is held 1 hour past the reserved time. After that, reception may release it; payments are refunded.

Flexible payment

Pay in full, pay a downpayment of any amount, or pay later. The Admin sets every room and extension price.

Ready means cleaned

After check-out, a room goes through inspection and cleaning or maintenance. Reception marks it done before the next guest enters.

For the safety and security of all guests and the protection of company property, please follow the check-in and check-out procedures at all times.
